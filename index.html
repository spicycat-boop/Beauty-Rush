How to Play ♡

① Read your client's request. Every client wants a different makeup look.

② Choose one product in each of six categories: Complexion, Cheeks, Eyeshadow, Lashes, Brows, and Lips.

③ Check your makeup bag. You can go back and change products before you finish a look.

④ Finish all five clients. Earn up to 100 points per client, for a total of 500 points.

⑤ Beat the clock! You have five minutes for the whole game. The timer only starts when you begin.
